# Brief: landing page for Loam

Design and build the landing page for Loam, a countertop composter for small kitchens. The buyer is someone who
cooks, lives in a flat and has given up on composting once already. The page should make the thing feel calm,
well made and worth $349. Use the copy below exactly; the design is yours.

Copy (use every line verbatim, add no other visible text):
- Brand: `Loam`
- Headline: `Compost without the compost heap.`
- Subline: `Loam turns a week of kitchen scraps into a cup of dry, odourless soil food. Overnight, on your counter.`
- Button: `Reserve yours`
- Price line: `$349. Ships in May.`
- Feature 1: `Quiet enough for a studio flat` / `Runs at 28 dB, about as loud as a fridge.`
- Feature 2: `Nothing to empty for a week` / `The 3-litre bucket takes what two people cook in seven days.`
- Feature 3: `No smell, no flies` / `A sealed lid and a carbon filter you replace twice a year.`
- Quote: `I stopped apologising for my bin.` / `Mara, Rotterdam`
- Footer: `Loam Home B.V., Amsterdam`

Hard requirements:
- One file, `index.html`, in the repository root. Everything is inside it: no other files, no network requests,
  no photo or font files. Illustrations in CSS or inline SVG are allowed and wanted.
- Every line of the copy appears exactly as written; no other visible text.
- The page works at 1440 and at 390 CSS pixels width with no horizontal scrolling.
- The button is a real `<a>` or `<button>` and is visible without scrolling at both widths (window heights 900
  and 844).
- No errors in the browser console.
