# Brief: launch copy for Loam

Write the launch copy for Loam from the fact sheet below. The reader cooks, lives in a flat and has given up on
composting once already. Sound like a person who likes the product, not like an advertisement. Use only facts
from the sheet.

Fact sheet:
- Loam is a countertop composter made by Loam Home B.V., Amsterdam.
- It dries and grinds kitchen scraps overnight (about 8 hours) into a dry, odourless soil additive.
- Bucket: 3 litres, about a week of scraps for two people. Volume shrinks by about 85 %.
- Noise: 28 dB. Sealed lid; carbon filter replaced twice a year.
- Energy: about 0.8 kWh per cycle.
- Not for: large bones, liquids, cooking oil.
- Price: $349. Reservations open now; ships in May.

Hard requirements:
- One file, `copy.md`, in the repository root, with exactly these three sections in this order:
  `## Headline` (one line, at most 60 characters), `## Summary` (exactly three sentences),
  `## Post` (100 to 130 words, plain text, no hashtags, no emoji).
- The name `Loam` and the price `$349` each appear in the summary and in the post.
- No number, claim or feature that is not on the fact sheet.
